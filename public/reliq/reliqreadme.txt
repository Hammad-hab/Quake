Title                   : Reliquary
Filename                : reliq.zip
Author                  : Michael Markie
Email Address           : mikedm92@gmail.com
Version:		: Alpha Version 1.0
Description             : A Quake mod based off of Copper featuring rebalanced weapons and very challenging levels.

* Play Information *

Single Player           : Yes
Cooperative             : Yes
Deathmatch              : No
Difficulty Settings     : Yes (Normal, Hard, Very Hard, Nightmare)

* New Features *

-Axe now does double damage and has knockback
-Pistol replacing the shotgun. Higher rate of fire and narrower spread.
-Runic Shotgun replacing double barrel. Triple barrels, very high damage and spread, slow reload.
-Both Nailguns rate of fire increased.
-Both rockets and grenades do very slightly more damage. No more 3 nade gibbing ogres. They will always die in 2.

* Future Versions *
-More Maps
-New enemies
-New weapons
