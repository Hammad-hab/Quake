===========================================================================
Title                   : The Punishment Due
Filename                : pun.zip
Release date            : 2021-03-15
Author                  : Julian 'Juz' Priestley
Email Address           : juzley@gmail.com

Description             :
  A 5 map (+ start map) episode for Quake, based on Lunaran's Copper Quake mod. No particular theme, no story, just go and kill some monsters!

Maps			:
	start.bsp - Start for Start's Sake (juzley)
	pun1.bsp - Azure Annoyance (juzley)
	pun2.bsp - Let Them Eat Quake (fairweather/juzley)
	pun3.bsp - Tour de Vores (juzley)
	pun4.bsp - With Fiends Like These (juzley)
	pun5.bsp - Ogre and Out (juzley)


Thanks to   :
Lunaran - The base copper mod, plus enduring my questions and fixing the couple of bugs I found in the copper code.
Alekswithak - Music for start.bsp
Dwell Team - Playtesting + general support.
Fairweather - Initial work on pun2.bsp
MarkieMusic - Some additional ambient sounds.
Various texture artists - I pulled textures and skyboxes in from a few places, and I have no idea who made what (sorry). If you see your textures here, thanks!

* Play Information *

Single Player           : Yes
Cooperative 2-4 Player  : Yes - tested with 2 players, but more should be possible.
	Look out for extra enemies, and a few places where some cooperation is required for buttons etc.
	There are a few rough edges, as this pack was designed firstly for single player and then
	retrofitted for coop, but overall it should play well in coop.
Deathmatch 2-4 Player   : No
Difficulty Settings     : Yes
